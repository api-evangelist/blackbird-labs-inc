# Blackbird Labs Inc

> Blackbird Labs is the company behind Blackbird, a payments-and-loyalty app for restaurants. Diners earn status, perks, and the $FLY rewards token by checking in and paying at partner restaurants; the settlement layer, Flynet, is a layer-three protocol built on Coinbase's Base. Blackbird is a consumer application — it does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI description, or developer portal.
